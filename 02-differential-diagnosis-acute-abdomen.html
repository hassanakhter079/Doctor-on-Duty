Ch. 17: General Surgery Acute Abdomen Book p. 415 Topic #02

Differential Diagnosis and Causes of Acute Abdomen

Master diagnostic differential matrix categorizing acute abdomen into gastrointestinal vs non-gastrointestinal causes across all anatomical abdominal quadrants.

Clinical Management & Pharmacotherapy Pakistani Standards

Accurate anatomical localization is the single most valuable initial discriminator in acute abdomen. Below is the systematic Gastrointestinal vs Non-Gastrointestinal etiology matrix:

Anatomical Quadrant Gastrointestinal Causes Non-Gastrointestinal Causes
Right Upper Quadrant (RUQ)
  • Acute cholecystitis & biliary colic
  • Cholelithiasis & Choledocholithiasis
  • Acute ascending cholangitis
  • Hepatitis & Acute hepatic congestion
  • Pyogenic / Amoebic liver abscess
  • Duodenal ulcer / Perforated ulcer
  • Right lower lobe pneumonia / pleurisy
  • Pulmonary embolism (RLL)
  • Subphrenic abscess
  • Right pyelonephritis / Renal calculus
  • Fitz-Hugh-Curtis syndrome (perihepatitis)
  • Herpes zoster (thoracic dermatome)
Epigastrium
  • Peptic ulcer disease (Gastric / Duodenal)
  • Acute pancreatitis
  • GERD / Severe gastritis
  • Early acute appendicitis (visceral pain phase)
  • Mallory-Weiss tear / Boerhaave syndrome
  • Gastric outlet obstruction
  • Acute Coronary Syndrome (Inferior MI)
  • Acute pericarditis
  • Aortic dissection (thoracoabdominal)
  • Rupturing Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm (AAA)
  • Pneumonia / Pleurisy
Left Upper Quadrant (LUQ)
  • Acute gastritis & gastric ulcer
  • Acute pancreatitis (tail pathology)
  • Splenic flexure colitis / diverticulitis
  • Splenic infarction or rupture / hematoma
  • Splenic abscess
  • Left lower lobe pneumonia / pleurisy
  • Left pyelonephritis / Renal colic
  • Myocardial infarction
Right Lower Quadrant (RLQ)
  • Acute appendicitis (most common)
  • Cecal diverticulitis
  • Meckel's diverticulitis
  • Terminal ileitis (Crohn's disease, Yersinia)
  • Mesenteric adenitis (especially children)
  • Incarcerated / strangulated right hernia
  • Right ureteric colic / Pyelonephritis
  • Ruptured ectopic pregnancy (life-threatening!)
  • Right ovarian torsion / Ruptured ovarian cyst
  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID) / Salpingitis
  • Endometriosis
  • Testicular torsion / Epididymo-orchitis
Left Lower Quadrant (LLQ)
  • Acute diverticulitis (sigmoid colon)
  • Sigmoid volvulus
  • Ulcerative colitis / Infectious colitis
  • Ischemic colitis
  • Incarcerated / strangulated left hernia
  • Left ureteric colic / Pyelonephritis
  • Ruptured ectopic pregnancy
  • Left ovarian torsion / Ruptured cyst
  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)
  • Endometriosis
  • Testicular torsion / Epididymo-orchitis
Suprapubic / Pelvic
  • Acute appendicitis (pelvic anatomical position)
  • Diverticulitis
  • Distal bowel obstruction
  • Acute urinary retention (distended bladder)
  • Acute cystitis / Prostatitis
  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease / Endometritis
  • Pregnancy complications / Dysmenorrhea
Diffuse / Generalized
  • Generalized secondary peritonitis (perforated viscus)
  • Acute mesenteric ischemia
  • Mechanical bowel obstruction (advanced)
  • Gastroenteritis
  • Paralytic ileus
  • Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)
  • Acute intermittent porphyria
  • Addisonian crisis / Adrenal insufficiency
  • Uremia
  • Familial Mediterranean Fever (FMF)
  • Ruptured Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm (AAA)
